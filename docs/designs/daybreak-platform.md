## Vision

### 10x Check
The 10x version of Daybreak is not a fast news feed with AI enrichment — it is the **institutional-grade AI analyst seat** for every Trillium trader. It actively manages trader attention, quantifies portfolio impact in real-time dollars, validates its own directional signals against historical price moves, ingests regulatory filings before news wires pick them up, and generates precedent-matched scenarios for macro traders thinking in analogies. Eventually it exposes all of this as a data API for Trillium's quant team to pipe into trading models.

### Platonic Ideal
Every Trillium trader feels like the best buy-side analyst in the world is sitting next to them — one who has read every article, remembers every precedent, knows the trader's book, and proactively surfaces what matters *to that trader specifically* before they even know to ask. They never miss a market-moving event. They never waste attention on noise. They understand second and third-order effects before their counterparties do.
